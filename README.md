# Gap Geometry

**D. B. — independent researcher, Belgium — January 2026 – present**

One number, held as an object. Take a quantity and its double: their geometric mean falls short of their logarithmic mean — always, and on the doubling interval by exactly G = 1 − √2·ln 2 ≈ 1.974 % of the latter. That shortfall is the gap — an exact number, computable to any depth, provably never zero. The ratio itself, p = √2·ln 2, is the value at ln 2 of one classical function, p(L) = L/(2 sinh(L/2)); the framework is the exact mathematics around that point, including where independent fields already stand at the same value. Researched, verified and corrected in open collaboration with AI systems — roles documented, every computation checkable.

## How to read a claim

Every claim carries its **kind** (theorem · reading · placement · conjecture), its **scope** (where it lives on the line, in the coordinate named) and its **status** (verified · observation · near-exact · reread). No numbered levels, no ladder. Symbols follow ISO 80000-2. The [Methodology](https://gap-geometry.github.io/sqrt2-ln2-geometric-constants-/methodology.html) page is the reference.

## Verification

Every number on the pages and in the papers is computed in arbitrary precision (mpmath, dps 500 as the inspection floor) and checked at two widely separated depths; a default float is only ever the thing under test. Every paper carries a reproduction block. The mathematics does not require trust — it requires a calculator. Start at [AI Readers](https://gap-geometry.github.io/sqrt2-ln2-geometric-constants-/ai-readers.html): one block, one second, dps 500.

## Deposits

All papers are deposited on OSF with DOIs — main project [osf.io/zx4g7](https://osf.io/zx4g7). OSF is the frozen anchor; this repository is the living layer. Corrections are additive and dated, never silent: a dated notice on the paper, the prior version kept.
